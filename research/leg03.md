## Key points

- **Continuity:** Karl's team said explicitly that each season resumed at the previous stopping point. In 2007: "make their way back to Uelen in order that Karl can continue the walk from the point it left off" (S33). Repositioning flights, ATV rides and the winter-road lift from Pevek to Dvoynoye were not counted as progress. In Aug 2007 he restarted at Amguema rather than Egvekinot because he had already walked through Amguema in spring; the Amguema → Egvekinot stretch is therefore a dead-end spur (s03-005).
- **Route changes:** Before the 2007 season the plan was Uelen → Egvekinot → Anadyr → Markovo → Magadan (S5). The late start and the 2-month permit forced the northern route through Komsomolsky (near Pevek), Bilibino and the Kolyma (S36). After Yakutsk he went south, not west: he followed the Lena/Amur highways to Neryungri and Amur oblast, then into Mongolia. He was not walking a "Yakutsk → Baikal" line. No source names Irkutsk, Baikal, Ulan-Ude or Kyakhta in connection with him.
- **The 2015 walk in the brief is really 2013–14.** The LA → DC protest walk started in Sept 2013 (Men's Journal; bushby3000.com showed him in Death Valley in Oct 2013) and was near Richmond VA in late Aug 2014. The new visa/invitation came on 23 Oct 2014. "2015" is the date of the Outside / National Geographic documentary coverage.
- **Visa bans:** (1) 2006: expulsion plus a five-year ban ordered by the Lavrentiya court, overturned on appeal 5 May 2006. (2) Visa refused 12 Apr 2012, followed by a five-year entry ban. US press in 2012 reported the ban; the Hull Daily Mail reported it in March 2013. The cause was straying into a closed border zone in 2011, which matches the March 2011 Nizhnekolymsky permit protocol. The ban was lifted 23 Oct 2014.
- **COVID:** none in this leg.
