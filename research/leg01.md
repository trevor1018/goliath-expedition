# Leg 01 — Punta Arenas (Nov 1998) → Darién Gap → Panama (Apr 2001)

## 1. Summary of the evidence base

The key find is **Karl Bushby's own day-by-day journals**. They were published 2000–2003 in the members' area of the official site `goliathexpedition.com` (also served as `earthtrekuk.net`) and archived by the Wayback Machine.

- **Extent:** 62 journal pages, covering 31 Oct 1998 – 25 Apr 2001 almost without a break.
- **Grid coordinates:**
  - Many daily headers carry a "Grid :" or "NN degrees NN minutes" position.
  - Systematic for Nov 1998 – Jan 1999 (Patagonia).
  - Intermittent in Chile, Peru and Ecuador.
  - Nearly daily again from 14 Jan 2001 (Darién / Panama).
  - These positions are used as `coord_basis: exact_reported`. Obvious typos are flagged.
- **Other material from the same sites:**
  - The home team's "Darien Gap" special pages (Thegap1–7).
  - Press releases PR1–PR8.
  - A dated "Latest Update" log.
- **2006 extracts:** `goliath.mail2web.com/Journals/*.asp` (2006 snapshots) holds curated extracts of the same diaries. They were checked and agree with the 2002 pages; they are cited as corroboration (S33, S36).

**Verification method:** every `quote` in `leg01.json` was checked automatically against the downloaded page text, after whitespace and curly-quote normalisation. All 33 quotable sources pass. Where a waypoint, segment or pause text shows a short phrase in quotation marks, that phrase was also checked against the corpus.

**Coverage method:** journal text was read in full for Argentina. For Chile, Peru, Ecuador/Colombia and Darién/Panama it was read via parallel extraction passes, each returning dated place lists with verbatim quotes. Key quotes from those passes were re-verified (see above).

**Not consulted:** *Giant Steps* (2006). The Internet Archive copy (`giantstepsameric0000bush`) is access-restricted lending only, and its search-inside endpoint returned "Item not available". Wikipedia's Darién / Metetí statement cites this book.

## 2. Route narrative (all dates from journals unless noted)

### Chile (Magallanes) → Argentina, Nov 1998
- **Arrival:** flew UK → Ascension → Falklands (31 Oct 1998), then to Punta Arenas airport, then a taxi into town.
- **First move:** on the night of 31 Oct/1 Nov he assembled "the beast" (two-wheeled trailer). After about 20 yards the wheels and axle failed: "Day one, year one … I was immobile" (PR1 / journal).
- **First walking day:** after repairs at a bike shop, "finally underway at 10:36" on **Mon 2 Nov 1998**.
- **4–5 Nov:** took a wrong dirt track north, then backtracked. This is walked, not transport, so no break in footsteps.
- **6–8 Nov:** regained the paved road on 6 Nov. Passed a refinery junction and a "ghost town" with rusting trawlers; our inference is San Gregorio.
- **9 Nov 1998:** crossed the border into Argentina at about 13:30. The post is unnamed. The grid of 52°00'S 69°31'W fits Monte Aymond / Integración Austral; that identification is an inference.

### Argentina, Nov 1998 – Mar 1999
- **Río Gallegos:** 11–15 Nov.
- **Comandante Luis Piedra Buena:** 20–27 Nov; rest for an injured toe.
- **Dirt road west to Gobernador Gregores:** arrived 4 Dec, left 9 Dec.
- **Ruta 40:** "now on route 40" from 11 Dec. Continued via **Bajo Caracoles** (about 17–18 Dec) to **Perito Moreno**, 22 Dec – 4 Jan (Christmas).
- **North on Ruta 40:** to **Río Mayo**, 9–20 Jan, staying with Gabriel. He was worried about his 90-day visa.
- **Gobernador Costa:** 28–31 Jan. **Tecka:** 2 Feb, where he cut his wrist.
- **Esquel, 5–22 Feb (funding pause):**
  - He walked off the main road down into Esquel.
  - He ran out of money ("Having now run out of money it was 'Cuppa Soup' for breakfast", 14 Feb) and waited for cash by post from his parents. It arrived 16 Feb.
  - **7 Feb (offroute):** visa run. A vehicle took him to Trevelin, he hitch-hiked to a remote frontier post south of Trevelin, crossed into Chile and back for a new stamp, and took a jeep back to Esquel.
  - **22 Feb (offroute):** William drove him back "to where I had left the main road to walk down to Esquel. As I had an already walked this route, I didn't need to do it again." This explicitly shows the continuity rule at work.
- **El Hoyo** 26 Feb, **El Bolsón** 27 Feb, **Bariloche** 4–7 Mar, **Villa La Angostura** 10 Mar.
- **Border:** Argentine post 11 Mar. He reached the "WELCOME TO CHILE" sign at the pass on **12 Mar 1999**; the Chilean checkpoint is 22 km downhill. The pass is not named; Paso Cardenal Samoré is our inference.

### Chile, Mar – Nov 1999
- **South (Mar–Apr):**
  - **Entre Lagos** 14 Mar; **Osorno** 16 Mar ("Total distance to date = approx 1370 miles").
  - From Osorno he followed **Route 5 / Pan-American** north.
  - Los Lagos 19 Mar, "broke". Lanco; **Temuco** 5–7 Apr, waiting for money; Victoria.
  - **Chillán** about 22–26 Apr; Longaví.
  - **1–3 May (offroute):** a farm visit by truck.
- **Central (May–Jul):**
  - San Fernando; Rancagua (money).
  - **Santiago** 12 May to about 28–31 May.
  - El Melón, where his girlfriend "Treiz" walked 15 km with him.
  - **Los Vilos** 10–14 Jun; Huentelauquén; **Coquimbo/La Serena** 22 Jun – 10 Jul; **Vallenar** 16–28 Jul.
- **North (Aug–Nov):**
  - **Copiapó** 1–9 Aug: visa overstay, paid US$100.
  - Balneario Flamenco; **Antofagasta** 30 Aug ("2740 mls"). He left Route 5 at La Negra to reach the city.
  - The pages for 31 Aug – 14 Sep are **missing**: the archived p.12 duplicates p.9.
  - Then the coastal road: Tropic of Capricorn 15 Sep (grid garbled), Mejillones area, Michilla, **Tocopilla** about 22–26 Sep.
  - **Iquique** about 1–20 Oct ("3080 mls"). He rejoined Route 5 at Humberstone; then Huara and Cuya.
  - **Arica** 29 Oct – 15 Nov, with offroute trips: a bus to Tocopilla and back, and a visa run to the border and the Chilean consulate in Tacna.
- **Into Peru:** walked across on the Pan-American on **15 Nov 1999**, about 13:00. The post is unnamed.

### Peru, Nov 1999 – Jun 2000 (Pan-American, coastal)
- **South:**
  - Tacna 16 Nov. Moquegua 23–26 Nov, a 5 km detour off the highway and back.
  - **Arequipa** 2 Dec 1999 – 4 Jan 2000. It was "well off my route" but he walked in. About a month's pause: money, Christmas.
  - **4 Jan 2000:** walked to Uchumayo, then a **truck lift (offroute)** to a Pan-American junction (probably La Repartición). It is **not established** that this was the junction he left on 1 Dec, so there is a possible unwalked gap.
- **Centre:**
  - Camaná, Ocoña, Chala, Nasca.
  - **Ica** 26 Jan – 7 Feb: waited for Western Union money; ill.
  - Chincha. His visa expired about 11 Feb.
  - **Lima** 17 Feb – 8 Mar (visa). He got only a 7-day extension and went on without a visa.
- **North:**
  - Huacho, Huarmey; **Chimbote** 21–27 Mar; Virú; **Trujillo** 2–7 Apr; Guadalupe, Mocupe.
  - **Chiclayo** 14–30 Apr: anaemia diagnosed. He made a Holy Week trip to Trujillo by truck and bus (offroute).
  - Mochumí, Motupe, Olmos; **Piura** 10–16 May (money).
  - **Sullana** about 17–25 May: bought a donkey and abandoned "the beast". What happened to the trailer is unknown.
- **Detour to Ecuador:** a deliberate 146 km inland detour via Las Lomas, Montero and Jililí, with a donkey and then a horse, "to try and avoid border police… It's the old visa problem again".
- **Into Ecuador:** an unofficial river crossing (the "River Caiuas", probably Río Calvas) on **3 Jun 2000**.

### Ecuador, Jun – Sep 2000
- **South:**
  - Cariamanga: sold the horse and sent his kit ahead by bus. Gonzanamá, Catamayo.
  - **Loja** 10–25 Jun. Offroute bus to Macará for a visa: paid Peruvian police US$20 and got 2 months. Also a taxi trip to Vilcabamba.
  - Saraguro, Cumbe; **Cuenca** 1–10 Jul ("5010 miles approx").
- **Centre (10–22 Jul):** Azogues, Cañar, El Tambo, Chunchi, Alausí, Guamote, Riobamba, Mocha, Ambato, Machachi. The journal for these days was rebuilt from memory: the originals were stolen in a Quito bar fight on 11 Aug.
- **Quito:**
  - **23 Jul:** driven about 20 km from north Quito to San Antonio / Mitad del Mundo (offroute). He stayed with the Imbaquingo family until 31 Aug; money was short.
  - Late Aug (date unknown) he walked that 22 km "from the point where I had been picked up".
- **North:** San José de Minas, Otavalo, Ibarra, Mira, El Ángel; **Tulcán** 7 Sep ("back onto the Pan-American").
- **Into Colombia:** walked across the border bridge on **10 Sep 2000** without stamps. The bridge is unnamed; Rumichaca is our inference.

### Colombia to Medellín, Sep – Nov 2000
- **South (Pan-American):**
  - Ipiales; **Pasto** 12–18 Sep.
  - Remolino, Mojarras, El Bordo (ELN roadblock reported), Rosas, **Popayán** 25–26 Sep.
  - Piendamó, Mondomo, Santander de Quilichao; walked with an army patrol 30 Sep – 1 Oct.
- **Cali** 2–16 Oct ("5,680 miles approx"). He stayed at a friend's house above the city (offroute) and on 16 Oct took a bus back to the centre to resume.
- **North:** Palmira, Buga, Zarzal, Cartago, Pereira; police and army checkpoints; Caldas.
- **Medellín**, 1 Nov 2000, "the anniversary of my second year".
- **Pause until 14 Jan 2001:**
  - Planning the Darién route ("I chose the Red Route", around Christmas).
  - Waiting for the dry season (PR5: "not expected to move until the wet season ends in January").
  - Funding: "my money had run out, but luckily mother had collected more".

### Darién Gap, 14 Jan – 16 Feb 2001
Primary sources: journals Colombia 5–7 and Panama 1–2; home-team summary Thegap3.

- **Approach (14–25 Jan):**
  - Left Medellín 14 Jan, disguised as a vagrant, on the **Medellín–Turbo road**.
  - San Jerónimo; Río Cauca / Santa Fe de Antioquia.
  - **Cañasgordas** 19 Jan: held briefly by the AUC.
  - Uramita 20 Jan; Dabeiba 21 Jan; questioned by FARC north of Dabeiba 22 Jan.
  - **Mutatá** 23 Jan; **El Tigre** 25 Jan, where he left the main road.
- **Detour to Riosucio (26–29 Jan):**
  - 26 Jan: a track west hit a dead end at "Casa 40".
  - 27 Jan: an AUC pick-up drove him back (offroute backtrack: "it hasn't advanced me one yard"), then he went on to the **Belén de Bajirá** army post.
  - 28 Jan: armed men passed his camp at night.
  - **Riosucio** army post 29–31 Jan.
- **Río Atrato and the border (1–13 Feb):**
  - 1–4 Feb: floated and swam down the **Río Atrato** hidden among debris, refusing boat lifts. The home team says about 32 miles in the river.
  - Left the river at Travesía; crossed jungle hills near Cristales / Bijao.
  - **12 Feb:** reached the border ridge about 300 m from his marked "Palo de Letras".
  - **13 Feb:** **Paya**, Panama: "Welcome to Panama, welcome to Central America and welcome to 'Stage Three'."
- **Companions:** none. He was alone throughout; locals showed him tracks.
- **No Gulf of Urabá boat:** he did not go via Turbo.

### Detention in Panama, 16 Feb – 7 Mar 2001
- **16 Feb:** reached **Boca de Cupe** via Pucuro and was detained. He had no Panamanian visa and no Colombian exit stamp.
- **Custody: 18 days in three places.** Both Karl and the home team give 18 days.
  - Boca de Cupe, 16–20 Feb.
  - Police cells at **Metetí**, 20–23 Feb. The police helicopter force-landed at Yaviza and a truck took him on. Karl: "I was far better off in Meteti."
  - **La Palma prison**, 23 Feb – 5 Mar.
- **Release:** immigration let him walk on and collect his visa in Panama City. He refused to fly.
- **Return to his stopping point:** 5–7 Mar, by boat, minibus, lifts and boats back to Boca de Cupe.
- **Restart, 8 Mar:** a police captain signed his notebook as proof of the restart point.
- **Deported?** No. There is no evidence of deportation.

### Panama to the Costa Rica border, Mar – Apr 2001
- **To Panama City:** jungle tracks to **Yaviza**, then the **Pan-American Highway**: Canglón, Metetí 10 Mar, Tortí, the Bayano lakes, Cañita.
- **Panama City** 16 Mar. The home team says "6,671 miles from his starting point". Pause until 4 Apr: no money, depression, equipment resupply at the British Embassy.
- **West:** Bridge of the Americas 4 Apr; La Chorrera, Penonomé, Natá, Chiriquí; **David** about 18–24 Apr.
- **25 Apr 2001:** "a few K's from the border with Costa Rica". The Costa Rica crossing belongs to leg 02.

## 3. Pauses and funding
- **Pattern:** there were many waits for money from home (by post, then Western Union): Esquel, Los Lagos, Temuco, Rancagua, Copiapó, Iquique, Arequipa, Ica, Piura, Quito, Medellín and Panama City.
- **Early warning:** "my money will run out in February" (21 Nov 1998). It did, at Esquel (14–16 Feb 1999).
- **Biggest pauses:**
  - Arequipa: about 1 month.
  - Lima (visa): 3 weeks.
  - Quito / San Antonio: about 5.5 weeks.
  - Medellín: about 2.5 months.
  - Panama City: about 2.5 weeks.
- **No trip to the UK:** none was found anywhere in this leg. All off-route travel was local: visa runs, social visits, and the police transfers in Panama.

## 4. Conflicts (also in JSON)
1. **Start date.**
   - The official site, press releases and Wikipedia say **1 Nov 1998**.
   - The journal shows a first 20-yard move and a breakdown on the night of 31 Oct / 1 Nov, and real walking from **2 Nov 1998, 10:36**.
   - Wikipedia also says he "left Hull on 5 October 1998". That is outside this leg's scope; the journal places him in the Falklands on 31 Oct.
2. **Metetí jail.** Wikipedia says "18 days in a jail in Metetí". Primary sources split the 18 days between Boca de Cupe, Metetí and mainly La Palma.
3. **Countries.** Wikipedia's list ("Argentina, Peru, Ecuador, and Colombia") omits Chile, where he walked for about 8 months.
4. **Garbled grids:**
   - 2 Nov 1998, longitude 71°50'W.
   - 21 Dec 1998, "41 degrees".
   - 15 Sep 1999, Tropic of Capricorn at 23°50'S.
   - 3 Jun 2000, "70 degrees 50" west.
   - Darién/Panama entries 3 Feb, 14 Mar and 13 Apr 2001 (not used as waypoints).
5. **Distance at Panama City.** "6,671 miles" is single-sourced (Thegap3).
6. **Secondary-web claims seen in search snippets but not used:**
   - Darién crossing "in 2000" (siakapkeli.my, a Malaysian article).
   - The crossing took "nearly four years" (a garbled search-summary line).
   - Both contradict the primary journals.

## 5. Open questions / gaps
- **Unnamed border posts and roads:** names of all border posts are inferred, not stated. Road numbers are given only for Ruta 40 (Argentina), Route 5 / Pan-American (Chile), and "Pan-American" (Peru, Colombia south of Cali, Panama).
- **Esquel junction:** where exactly he left the main road for Esquel.
- **Antofagasta:** departure date and route, 31 Aug – 14 Sep 1999. Journal p.12 was never captured correctly; an earthtrekuk.net copy, if one exists, was not found.
- **Possible unwalked gaps:**
  - Arequipa: was the Pan-American junction he was dropped at on 4 Jan 2000 the same one he left on 1 Dec 1999?
  - Chile farm visit (1–3 May 1999): did he resume at the exact point?
  - Arica trips (Nov 1999): same question.
- **Undated events:** the Loja → Macará visa trip and the Quito → San Antonio make-up walk.
- **The trailer:** what happened to "the beast" after May 2000 (switch to donkey, then horse, then backpack with kit sent ahead by bus).
- ***Giant Steps* (2006):** it may refine or correct journal details. Recommend checking a physical or e-book copy (ISBN 0316729582 / 9780751536959 / ebook 9781405521024).
