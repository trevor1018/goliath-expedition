# Leg 05 — Baku → Georgia → Turkey → Europe → Channel (swim) → England → Hull

Research date: 2026-11-01. **Status: the expedition is NOT finished as of today.** The Hull finish is scheduled for **Monday 2 Nov 2026**. On 31 Oct he was in Lincolnshire, "embarking on his final few miles" (BBC). Re-check after 2 Nov.

## Narrative (sourced; source IDs refer to leg05.json)

### Azerbaijan → Georgia (Nov 2024 – Feb 2025)
- **Leaving Baku.** A Facebook post dated 9 Nov 2024 reads "Leaving Baku and headed west. The last section of a 25 year life on the road begins." (S2). A search-engine summary says the official site gives **10 Nov 2024** (S3). I could not verify the site directly.
- **Georgia.** The only Georgian point sourced is **Batumi**, from a Facebook video dated 30 Jan 2025 (S2).
  - Not found: the Azerbaijan–Georgia border crossing used, whether he went through Tbilisi, and any dates inside Georgia.
  - Explorersweb (28 Apr 2025): "For the last six months, Bushby has been picking his way from Azerbaijan to Turkey" (S36).

### Turkey (Feb – Sep 2025, split in two)
- **Entry.** He entered at the **Sarp Border Gate** on 5 Feb 2025. A photo caption says 6 Feb, Artvin (S4).
- **Announced plan.** Hopa on day 1, the Black Sea coast to Samsun, then inland "over mountains and highways" to Istanbul, then "Greece via Edirne" (S4). The Greece part did not happen; he went into Bulgaria.
- **Samsun.** Reported around 13 Mar 2025 (S5, S2).
- **Bolu.** Instagram post on 16 Apr 2025, about 260 km east of Istanbul (S2).
- **Üsküdar.** He reached Üsküdar (Asian side) between 25 and 30 Apr 2025 (S3, S36, S7).
- **Bosphorus.** On **2 May 2025** he walked across the **15 July Martyrs Bridge** with special permission (S10, S1).
  - He left Turkey the same day because his 90-day stay was expiring.
  - He waited in **Mexico** (BBC, 11 Jun 2025, S38).
  - Wikipedia says "Armenia" and cites that same BBC piece, but the BBC piece says Mexico. Treat this as a Wikipedia error.
- **Return.** He flew back to Istanbul around 19 Aug 2025 (Explorersweb, S1) and walked to Edirne.
  - He left Turkey through **Kapıkule** on **1 Sep 2025** (S12).
  - Not found: the road he used from Istanbul to Edirne.

### Bulgaria → Romania → Hungary (Sep – Dec 2025)
- **Bulgaria.** He walked road 8 via **Haskovo, Plovdiv and Pazardzhik** to **Sofia**. He was in Sofia around 16–20 Sep 2025 and held a meet-up on 20 Sep (S13).
  - Stated next step: over the Stara Planina to **Vidin**, then "across the bridge over the Danube into Romania" (S13). This is a plan, not a confirmed crossing.
- **Romania.** He was in Romania in Oct 2025, about 2,213 km from the UK (Wikipedia, citing an archived westboundhorizons page, S14).
  - I found no account of his route through Romania.
  - **Serbia is not mentioned anywhere.**
- **Hungary.**
  - **Budapest** on 15 Nov 2025 (TikTok, cited by Wikipedia, S14/S15).
  - **Komárom** around 3 Dec 2025 (S16).
  - **Győr** "before Christmas" 2025 (BBC, S17).
- **Slovakia.** Noviny.sk speculated he was in Slovakia "possibly near Bratislava" (S16). This is not confirmed, and BBC quotes Bushby saying he would resume "close to the Hungary-Austria border". See conflicts.
- **Winter pause.** From Christmas 2025 to March 2026 he was in Mexico, for the Schengen 90/180-day rule (S17).

### Austria → Germany → Belgium (Mar – Jun 2026)
- **Austria.**
  - Mid-March 2026: "days away from crossing into Austria from Hungary" (BBC, 14 Mar, S18).
  - In **Vienna** he delivered the 1999 postcard (S19).
  - 24 Mar 2026: on the Danube path north of Vienna, past **Tulln** (S2).
- **Companion.** Angela Maxwell rejoined him in early April 2026 (S2).
- **Germany.**
  - In May 2026 he walked a 6-day, ~120 km stretch: "Fuchsstadt" → Waldbüttelbrunn → **Wertheim → Collenberg → Miltenberg → Obernburg** (Main valley) → **Frankfurt Airport** (Tripsite, S20).
  - "Fuchsstadt" is ambiguous: there is one in Landkreis Würzburg and one near Hammelburg. I have not plotted it.
  - Not found: where he crossed from Austria into Germany, and his route from Frankfurt to Belgium.
  - A search-engine summary mentioned footage near "Linz and Regensburg", but I could not trace the source. Not used.
- **Belgium.**
  - He entered Belgium about the last week of May 2026 (S2).
  - In mid-June 2026 he was in Belgium when **Eurotunnel refused** him access to the service tunnel. Eurotunnel said closing it "for a period of 15 hours, or over two days" would pose safety and maintenance risks (S22).
  - He then left Schengen (to Mexico, per Wikipedia, S14).
  - His stopping point in June is unknown.

### Belgium → France (Sep 2026)
- **Resumed in Belgium.** He restarted in early/mid September 2026.
  - BBC walked with him from **Havré to Mons** (S23).
  - In **Tournai** he arrived around 17 Sep and met Louis-Philippe Loncke on Fri 18 Sep (S24, S25).
  - The Tournai stop was unplanned, "Quelques démarches administratives" (administrative formalities); he needed a medical fitness certificate to swim.
- **France.** He entered France on **20 Sep 2026** according to Wikipedia only. The NoTélé article Wikipedia cites does not give that date.
  - I found no town-level route to the coast. Calais was the expected staging point (S22, S25).

### English Channel (≈2/3 – 5 Oct 2026) — mode: swim
- **Background.**
  - Permission came from French authorities, plus UK coordination: "HM Coastguard ... liaised with French authorities" (S26, S37).
  - BBC notes that France had for many years only tolerated swims departing from the UK.
  - The planned start was 1 Oct, tides and weather permitting (S37).
- **Start point and time.** Undisclosed. The team kept the departure location and time private "for safety and security reasons" (S26). The Calais-area coordinates in the JSON are a placeholder only.
- **Format.**
  - "20 hours over three days" in the water (S26, S28). BBC's 31 Oct piece also quotes "perfect three days" (S30).
  - He spent nights on a support boat and **restarted from his last position each morning** (Explorersweb, S27). This is the continuity claim.
  - Wikipedia says "approximately four days", which probably includes rest time.
- **Equipment.** "wetsuit, fins, paddles for my hands, a flotation vest, neck flotation", swimming on his back. Bushby calls it "floatimming" (Guardian, S28).
- **Distance.** 21 miles (34 km) per the Guardian.
- **Solo or relay.** Solo swimmer. No co-swimmer is named, unlike the Caspian swim. The support crew and boat are unnamed, and no pilot is named.
- **Landfall.** **Shakespeare Beach, just south of Dover, at 15:30 BST on Monday 5 Oct 2026** (BBC).
  - He was met by his father Keith, his brothers and friends, and by Werner Herzog, who is filming "Walk of Life" (S26).
  - The news was first published about 24 hours later (S14).
- **CSA / CS&PF.** I found **no record** of ratification by the Channel Swimming Association or the Channel Swimming & Piloting Federation.
  - My own general knowledge, not checked against the rulebooks: fins, hand paddles, flotation aids and a multi-day staged format fall outside the standard rules of both bodies.
  - Bushby himself disclaims it as "real" swimming.
  - Treat this as an unratified, permission-based staged crossing.

### England (8/9 Oct – 2 Nov 2026)
- **Dover.** He rested in Dover, including his first fish and chips in 28 years (S28, S30).
- **Start of the walk north.** About 8 Oct (Guardian: "Tomorrow, he'll start the walk home") or 9 Oct (Fox, S29). Angela Maxwell walked with him from Dover (S30).
- **Route Dover → Lincolnshire: unknown.**
  - I found no source for the Thames crossing, London, Kent towns or Cambridgeshire towns.
  - One spokesperson line (via Brit Brief, rewriting ITV) puts him "near Barton in South Cambridgeshire, with about 130 miles left" on Sat 31 Oct. This is incompatible with AP's report of him near **Market Rasen, Lincolnshire, on Wed 28 Oct, "about 30 miles from the finish line"** (S31). See conflicts.
- **Planned finish (Hull City Council, 26 Oct, S32):**
  1. Cross the **Humber Bridge**, then head east into Hull.
  2. Arrive at **Queen Victoria Square around 11:00 on Mon 2 Nov 2026**.
  3. Hull Volunteers welcome him; Lord Mayor Cllr Maria Coward brings him onto a stage at City Hall; a brief private reception follows.
  4. He then goes **north to his family home**, his mother Angela's house on the **Sutton Park estate**. That is the **official finish** (S32, S34).
- **Arrival status as of 2026-11-01:** not arrived. Do not mark the expedition complete until there is post-event reporting.
