## Narrative
1. **Mongolia (2017).** Karl crossed from Russia in 2017. The border post is not sourced (Kyakhta–Altanbulag would be the obvious one, but nothing says so). He reached Ulaanbaatar on **8 Aug 2017** (S2, S24). There he joined Baigalmaa Norjmaa's *Steppes to the West* project: 10 Bactrian camels, with Angela Maxwell and Jamie Ramsay among others. The camels carried the gear and he walked alongside (S3, S9). The animals were trained at a camp west of UB. The caravan left "just before winter" and covered "more than a thousand kilometres" (S2); Explorersweb says 1,130 km (S6). Norjmaa says they were "in the Gobi desert in mid-winter" after the first month (S11). Maxwell was with the project "From August to mid-December" 2017. By 25 Feb 2018 everyone except Norjmaa had left (S10). **Where the group split is not sourced.** Karl then walked on alone with his cart. On 31 May 2018 his father put him "a couple of hundred kilometres from the Chinese border" in desert (S12).
2. **China (2018).** He crossed into China alone in 2018. The crossing point is unknown, and so is whether he passed through Inner Mongolia/Gansu. In Xinjiang the police stopped him, confiscated his footage and told him the walk was over. They offered to drive him to the Kazakh border, which he refused because a ride would break the rule. After the British Embassy intervened he walked to the Kazakh border on a police-mandated route with a 24-hour escort and no camping (S2). His China route **ended at the Kazakh border by walking, under escort**. It did not end because of COVID or a visa.
3. **Kazakhstan → Uzbekistan → Turkmenistan (late 2018 / 2019).** Almaty, where he met his partner "Rika" → Uzbekistan, entered "early June 2019" according to an unsourced claim → Samarkand → Bukhara → Turkmenistan (S2, S8). He had heatstroke and spent several days in hospital (S2). He entered Turkmenistan alone on a 30-day visa with a government escort and crossed the Karakum to the Iranian border (late Aug–Sep 2019, or "end of 2019" in Karl's words). The Iran visa was refused. He was taken to Ashgabat and flown out (S1).
4. **Pause 2019–2023.** The causes were stacked:
   - Iran refused him as a UK passport holder, and US sanctions meant his US sponsors could not help with Iran.
   - The killing of Soleimani (Jan 2020) cut off his contacts.
   - COVID hit in spring 2020.
   - Turkmenistan closed its borders.
   - The Russia route closed after the Feb 2022 invasion of Ukraine.
   
   He waited in Mexico (S1, S2, S4).
5. **Continuity logic (the key point).** He did **not** resume at the Iran border. He went back to **Bukhara**, his "last major waypoint in Uzbekistan before we entered Turkmenistan", and walked a new line north-west from there. The Bukhara→Turkmenistan→Iran-border section (~700 miles by Karl's estimate) is therefore a dead-end spur that no longer counts (S1, S8: "all of the walking he did in Turkmenistan will have been for naught"). Explorersweb's "flew to Bukhara" (S6) is loose wording: S1 says they flew to Tashkent and took the train to Bukhara.
6. **Bukhara → Caspian (2023–24), with Angela Maxwell.** The dates and distances come from S1:
   - Bukhara 14 Mar 2023 → Nukus 543 km (by 8 Apr). This is the Kyzylkum crossing.
   - Visa break.
   - Nukus (June) → Kazakh border 441 km, past the Aral Sea.
   - Border → Beyneu 87 km (28 Jun).
   - Beyneu → Shetpe 309 km (7–18 Jul 2023).
   - Visa break plus the swim postponement.
   - Shetpe → swim start ~300 km (14–28 Apr 2024, with Erik Scheeres).
   
   The total is ≈1,680 km (≈1,045 mi), which matches the "1,000 mile section". Geographically, Nukus→Beyneu crosses Karakalpakstan and the Ustyurt plateau. That is our inference; S1 only mentions the Aral Sea. No source names the roads.
7. **Caspian swim (2024).**
   - **Start:** the coast "near Aktau" (S20, S21; Tengrinews says "from Aktau"). No source gives the exact beach. Open puzzle: Shetpe→start was "Roughly 300 km", which is more than Shetpe→Aktau by road (~150–160 km by general knowledge). The start point may lie south of Aktau.
   - **Start date:** planned 16 Aug (S15, S16). Karl posted on 16 Aug that the team was leaving for the start point (S8). Counting 31 days back from the finish gives ~17–18 Aug.
   - **Finish:** 17 Sep 2024, 18:30 (Karl, via S8). Landfall was welcomed at the **Sea Breeze resort** (Nardaran, Absheron, inside Baku's territory) (S19). Other outlets just say "in Baku".
   - **Not October.** The "October 2024" date in Explorersweb (S6) is an error. Their original swim article came out 19 Oct 2024.
   - **Method:** daily stages of about 3 h + 3 h, or a planned 10 km/day. The swimmers slept on the support boats. Karl calls his stroke "floatimming" and used a flotation vest and wetsuit (S25 AP via Korea Times).
   - **Support:** the Azeri Coast Guard plus a 64-m ship and/or an Aktau support boat. Vessel names are unknown.
   - **Not found:** no source explains how each day's end position was marked and resumed (GPS/SPOT). A SPOT live tracker existed but its data was not recovered.
   - **Team:** Angela Maxwell, Anastasiya Boborikina, Abdurrahman Rustamov.
   - **Funding:** Azerbaijan's Ministry of Youth and Sports and Baku City Circuit.

## Conflicts (summary — details in JSON)
- **Start month:** Aug 2024 (well supported) vs Oct 2024 (Explorersweb 2025, wrong).
- **Duration:** 31 days (Karl) vs 32 at sea / 27 swimming (Explorersweb, Sea Breeze) vs 41 (AZERTAC, idman, Kazakh press).
- **Distance:** 288 km vs 275.5 km (Karl's stats as quoted) vs ~300 km planned vs 259 km (2022 plan).
- **Support vessels:** described three different ways.
- **Year he entered Kazakhstan:** 2018 vs ~2019.
- **Date he reached the Iran border:** 27 Aug / 15 Sep / "end of 2019".
- **Camel trek:** Norjmaa's claim (unverified search snippet) that Karl quit after ~3 days, against ~1,000+ km per Karl.
- **WBH 2023 post dates:** internally inconsistent (27/03 vs 12/03).
- **Garbled secondary accounts:** Otello.ru has Karl returning to Latin America and later to Kazakhstan for Russia. Explorersweb 2025 places COVID in 2018.
