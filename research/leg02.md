# Leg 02: Panama (after the Darién Gap) to Uelen, Chukotka (April 2001 – March 2006, plus the detention aftermath)

## Narrative (sources in brackets)

### Panama to Guatemala (2001)
- **Panama:** He stayed in Panama City from 17 Mar to 4 Apr 2001. On 4 Apr he left before dawn over the Bridge of the Americas. He walked the Pan-American Highway west through La Chorrera (4 Apr), Penonomé (8 Apr) and Natá (9 Apr), reaching 08°08'N 82°06'W on 16 Apr. [S1]
- **Costa Rica:** The journal mentions San José only in hindsight. He crossed into Nicaragua on 20 May 2001. The border staff paid his entry fee themselves. [S1]
- **Nicaragua:** He went via Rivas and along the shore of Lake Nicaragua, reaching Jinotepe on 30 May and León on 3 June. In León he stopped to have a trailer built; how long he stayed is unknown. [S1]
- **Honduras:** There are no dated entries. The page header lists Honduras, and he changed "Honduran money" at the El Salvador border. [S1]
- **El Salvador:** San Miguel on 30 Jul, Usulután on 2 Aug, and 13°41'N 89°56'W on 9 Aug. [S1]
- **Guatemala:** On 30 Aug he was at 14°21'N 92°10'W, "within only 5k's of Mexico". [S1]

### Mexico (Aug 2001 – Apr 2002)
- **Border crossing:** He crossed from Tecún Umán to Ciudad Hidalgo on about 30–31 Aug 2001, then went to Tapachula. [S2]
- **Oaxaca:** Coastal route through Tehuantepec (late Sep) to coastal Oaxaca at 16°00'N 97°32'W on 10 Oct.
- **Gap:** From mid-Oct 2001 to Feb 2002 there is only one entry (24 Nov: "the road continues almost flat along the coast").
- **Pacific north:** Nayarit at 22°09'N 105°13'W on 5 Feb 2002, then Highway 15 (named) via Santiago Ixcuintla, Villa Unión, Mazatlán and Culiacán. Navojoa on 26 Mar, Nogales on 27–28 Apr. [S2]

### USA (Apr 2002 – Feb 2003)
- **Entry:** Nogales, Arizona, on 29 Apr 2002, with a 90-day visa waiver. [S3, S25]
- **Arizona:** He walked frontage roads and the Interstate to Tucson and Phoenix, then Wickenburg (~22 May). He then turned "south/south west for California": Parker (13 Jun), Lake Havasu City (17 Jun), and on to the I-40 junction.
  - The geography means he probably went through Needles, California on US-95 to reach Searchlight, but **no source states this**.
- **Nevada:** Searchlight on 25 Jun, then Las Vegas.
- **Visa trip (Wikipedia only):** He reached Las Vegas in July, flew to Bogotá for a new visa, and resumed at Las Vegas in August. Wikipedia cites Arizona Daily Star clippings that I could not open. [S25]
- **Interstate 15 north:** The journal resumes at Overton on 19 Aug, then follows I-15 ("my only route north from here"): Mesquite, Fillmore, and Nephi (exit 222, ~11 Sep). After Nephi the Highway Patrol made him leave I-15, so he used side roads through Provo to Salt Lake City (mid-Sep to Oct). He rejoined I-15 at Downey, Idaho, then went on to Idaho Falls (26 Oct), Rexburg (29 Oct) and Shelby, Montana. [S3]
- **Cart theft:** On 31 Dec 2002 he stopped at the bar at **I-15 Exit 379**, 19 miles south of the border. His cart "B2" was stolen just after midnight on New Year's. He got lifts to Conrad, Tracy and Helena, where a new trailer was built. Ruth Finch drove him back to Exit 379 on 13 Feb 2003. He walked to Sweetgrass/Coutts the same day and was given a one-year Canadian visa. [S3]

### Canada (Feb 2003 – May 2004)
- **Alberta, winter 2003:** Coutts, then Medicine Hat, then a deliberate detour north-east to BATUS at Suffield (about a week; left 1 Mar). He then walked to a truck stop near Bassano. On 7 Mar he was driven to Calgary (offroute) to meet his father; on 9 Mar he was driven back to the same truck stop and resumed. Gleichen came next, and he reached the Calgary outskirts on 14 Mar 2003. [S4]
- **Calgary pause (Mar–Jul 2003):** A long stay while his supporters fought Canadian immigration over his partner Catalina ("Catty") Estrada's visa, which was refused. He left on 24 Jul 2003. [S4]
- **Rockies:** Banff (27 Jul) and Lake Louise (31 Jul), walking with CBC journalist Kisha Ferguson, then Jasper (12–13 Aug). The route from Jasper to Grande Prairie is not documented. He was in Grande Prairie from 3 to 13 Oct, then Beaverlodge. [S4, S5]
- **Alaska Highway:** He started the Alaska Highway at Dawson Creek on 22 Oct 2003, then Fort St John (25 Oct). He stayed at Fort Nelson until 1 Jan 2004; arrival date and reason unknown. Then Watson Lake (8 Mar 2004, four days), Swift River Lodge (resupply), and Whitehorse (~1 Apr, about a week). He crossed into Alaska at Beaver Creek on 1–2 May 2004. [S4]

### Alaska (May 2004 – Feb 2006)
- **Into Fairbanks, 2004:** Dot Lake on 13–14 May 2004, then Fairbanks. The arrival date was not found. KUAC radio's story "reaches Alaska" ran on 11 Aug 2004. [S5, S24]
- **Winter 2004–05:** He left from Murphy Dome, near Fairbanks, on 16 Dec 2004 by snow-machine trail onto the Minto Flats (GPS 65°15.029'N 148°42.588'W). Manley Hot Springs on 10 Jan 2005 at −45 °C, then the Tanana area and the Yukon River, Ruby (~25 Feb), and the Kaltag portage. He met Dimitri Kieffer ("DK"), who was racing from Anchorage to Nome. They walked the last 36 miles together and reached **Unalakleet on 24 Mar 2005** at about 22:30. The home team's page gives 27 Mar instead. [S5, S9]
- **Season over:** His visa was about to expire and the ice was melting. He flew Unalakleet → Nome → Fairbanks, then went to Colombia, and was back in Alaska in early Oct 2005. [S5, S9]
- **Winter 2005–06, Unalakleet to Nome:** He prepared in Anchorage and Fairbanks, met Troy Henkels (whose own crossing attempt had failed), and flew back to Unalakleet in late Dec 2005; the exact date is unknown. Shaktoolik on 30–31 Dec. He then went straight across Norton Bay. On 3 Jan 2006 he was marooned on new ice; the troopers were alerted, but he got himself off. Then Koyuk, Elim, Golovin (14 Jan) and White Mountain, with the "notorious 30 miles" of Iditarod coast on 20 Jan. He reached Nome on an unknown date and met Kieffer there. [S6]
- **Drift off Cape Douglas (3–5 Feb 2006):** Their camp on the ice on the south side of Cape Douglas broke away in a storm. They woke "28 miles out to sea, south-east of King Island". An Evergreen helicopter lifted them off. Karl was **dropped 2 miles from Cape Douglas and walked back up to the Cape**, so his line stayed unbroken. Kieffer flew to Nome with level-3 frostbite on one finger and rejoined at Teller (9 Feb). [S6]
- **To Wales:** Lost River (18 Feb, GPS 65°23.732'N 167°15.285'W), then Cape Mountain. **Wales on 21 Feb 2006**, where they set up a base in the school. [S6]

### Bering Strait crossing (17–31 Mar 2006), with Dimitri Kieffer [S7, plus the S10 home-page update]
- **17 Mar:** They left Wales school, went along the coast to the north-west and onto the fast ice. About 3 miles out was the first moving ice. They **swam a lead "hundreds of metres wide"** towing two sleds, in immersion suits. Beyond it was "ice soup" where they repeatedly sank into the water. They drifted north at about 2 mph.
- **20 Mar:** The drift reversed south. They were "about midway along this 50mile giant 'teardrop' lead" and stayed put for the day.
- **21 Mar:** 5 miles west, per the home page (S10).
- **22 Mar:** Their best day, 8 miles west. First polar bear tracks. They considered a last helicopter resupply before the Date Line; **whether it happened is not stated**.
- **25 Mar:** At 04:00 the ice broke under the tent. They **crossed the Date Line into Russian waters** and made 4.6 miles. The ice behind them was turning into open water.
- **28–29 Mar:** They had been stuck about 20 miles offshore for 3 days and were short of food and fuel. An attempt to switch to backpacks failed.
- **30 Mar:** They crossed an ice "moat" and climbed a 20 ft wall onto the Russian fast ice, about 5 miles out, and saw Uelen. They stopped half a mile short of land at dark, on Karl's 37th birthday.
- **31 Mar:** Landfall in a white-out at an **abandoned early-warning radar base about 3 miles north-west of Uelen**. They walked into Uelen.
- **Not published:** No GPS track of the crossing has been published. All mid-strait waypoints are estimates (`approximate_region`).
- **Karl's continuity claim:** "There is not one yard between ... Punta Arenas ... and Uelen ... that I have not walked or swam." [S8]

### Aftermath (offroute)
- **Detention:** They reported to the Border Guard in Uelen [S11] and were detained, by AP's account on 1 Apr [S20]. The FSB said they were accused of illegal border crossing; they had a pistol, satellite phones and GPS [S19]. They were moved to **Lavrentiya**, restricted to the village, and stayed with the Orthodox priest Father Leonid. Their equipment was seized and their passports were returned unstamped. [S11, S15, S16]
- **Court case:** A Chukotka court (≈14 Apr 2006) fined them $72 each and ordered deportation with a 5-year ban [S20]. **On appeal (reported 5 May 2006) the expulsion was cancelled and a fine (£40) stood.** [S17, S25]
- **Leaving Russia:** They flew from Russia to Nome on **24 May 2006**, after 54 days in Russia [S21, S22, S23]. It was a departure, not a deportation. Karl then went to Colombia for the summer to get Chukotka permits [S12]. Walking resumed from Uelen in a later leg.

## Conflicts (details in JSON)
1. **Start point:** Wales [S7, S18, S26] or Nome [S21]. S21 is wrong.
2. **Length:** 14 days [S7, S13] or 15 days [S19, S20]. Both fit the same dates, counted differently.
3. **Arrival in Russia:** 31 Mar [S7, S19] or "2 April" [S16, S17]. The detention date of 1 Apr comes from S20.
4. **Distances:** The strait is given as 56 or 58 miles wide. The route actually walked is given as 150 or 200 miles.
5. **Swimming:** "5 days" comes only from Kieffer-side and newspaper sources [S21, S26]. The diary extracts explicitly describe only the day-1 swim, but they skip days.
6. **Drift and helicopter rescue:** S21's "36 h drift + helicopter rescue" is the February Cape Douglas incident, mixed into the crossing story.
7. **Where detained:** Uelen [S11, S25] or Lavrenty [S19]. Most likely they reported in Uelen and were held in Lavrentiya.
8. **Fine:** $72 [S20] or £40 [S17]. The figures come from different stages and conversions.
9. **Kieffer's nationality:** French [S13], American [S17], or both [S18].
10. **Unalakleet arrival in 2005:** 24 Mar [S5] or 27 Mar [S9].
11. **Detail:** The diary heading for 22 May 2002 gives 34°58'N 113°44'W, which doesn't fit "near Wickenburg". It is probably a typo, so I did not use it.
